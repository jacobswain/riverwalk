About

Skate the Riverwalk at this all-new ice rink inside Norfolk’s beloved Winterfest on the Wisconsin celebration. Glide under twinkling lights, surrounded by the charm of the holidays, festive music, and the joyful energy of the waterfront.

Whether you're sipping cocoa between laps, creating family traditions, or enjoying a romantic winter date, this open-air rink brings a classic holiday experience to the heart of Winterfest. Lace up and make memories on the ice all season long!

Tickets & Reservations

You can purchase tickets online ahead of time or on-site (first come, first served). Please note that your reservation is valid only for the date and time you select.

Each ticket includes 75 minutes of skating fun, starting when you check in at the rink.

After purchase, you'll receive two emails:

  • Your receipt, and
  • A link to your Reservation Management page, where you can add skater names and complete electronic waivers.

Each skater will need to complete their own waiver, and parents or guardians must sign for minors.

All skating sessions are weather permitting. While we do our best to have all skate sizes available, wait times may occur during busy periods. A waiver is required for every guest each time they skate.

More Information

Parking

Convenient parking is available in several Downtown Norfolk garages. Many offer two hours of free parking daily (except during the Grand Illumination Parade, when event rates apply). After two hours, standard City rates apply.

Garages Closest to the Rink:

  • Town Point Garage – 110 W. Main St.
  • The Main Garage – 100 E. Main St. (entrance on Plume St.; exit through The Main Hotel to Main St.)
  • Main Street Garage – 230 E. Main St.
  • West Plume Garage – 135 W. Plume St.

Parking is also available at the Waterside Garage (50 Martin's Lane), though rates may be slightly higher due to an existing lease.
